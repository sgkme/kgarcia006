# Snack Stack catalog download

Download `Snack-Stack-Catalog.zip`, extract it, and open the new `Snack-Stack-Catalog.html` in Chrome or Edge. Click **Watch the menu run**.

The single HTML file works offline and contains all 105 menu items, ingredient inspection and SVG downloads, artwork animations, the full animated menu run, and the flatter pizza perspective. The preview starts with Root Beer Float.

This recipe-audit update separates upper/lower layers into individual catches, fills drinks before adding toppings, and assembles flat tortillas before wrapping. Breakfast burritos have six ingredient steps. Waffles share the pancake perspective and syrup process; sandwich bread has rounded crust and porous crumb. Bowls and fries are fuller, enchilada cheese follows each roll, and cut faces remain attached to their food. Whole containers stay intact.

Order changes and tab navigation continue requested playback; explicit Pause stays paused. Validation served all 105 menu items, completed 315 engine runs across three difficulties, and rendered 8,680 intermediate frames across the 105 menu items and four kit recipes with no browser errors. Mouse, keyboard, touch, pause/resume, offline operation, and SVG downloads were checked too.

The ZIP includes `RECIPE-AUDIT.md` with the findings and corrections. It is also available beside this README.

The additional menu recipes are catalog-derived previews. The four original kit recipes remain in their own collection.
